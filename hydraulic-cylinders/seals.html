    Static and dynamic seals

    Static seal

    Seals a joint between two parts that do not move relative to each other, such as the barrel and the end caps. It is squeezed once when the cylinder is assembled and then just holds. O-rings and gaskets are typical.

    Dynamic seal

    Seals between surfaces that slide (or rotate) past each other, such as the rod through the head or the piston in the bore. It must hold pressure while moving, resist wear and friction, and leave only a thin film of oil. U-cups, vee packings, piston rings and wipers are typical.

    The test

    Ask whether the two surfaces move relative to each other, not whether the seal moves. The O-ring between the piston and the rod travels the full stroke, yet it is static: the piston and rod move together.
